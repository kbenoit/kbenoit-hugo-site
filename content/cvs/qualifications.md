### Academic Positions

Singapore Management University, School of Social Sciences. Dean, School of Social Sciences and Professor of Computational Social Science, 2024–present. Lee Kong Chian Professor of Computational Social Science, 2026–2027.

London School of Economics and Political Science, Department of Methodology, 2010–2024. Professor of Computational Social Science. Director, Data Science Institute, 2020–2024.

Australian National University, School of Politics and International Relations. Professor, part-time, 2018–2024.

Trinity College Dublin, Department of Political Science. Professor of Quantitative Social Sciences, 2007–2010; Professor (part-time), 2012–present; Associate Professor, 2005–2007; Fellow, 2003–present; Lecturer, 1998–2005. Head of Department of Political Science, 2008–2010.

Central European University, Budapest, Department of Political Science. Visiting Lecturer, 1997–1998.

### Education

Ph.D., Harvard University, Cambridge, MA. 1998. Department of Government. Supervisors: Gary King, Kenneth Shepsle, Grzegorz Ekiert.

M.A., Harvard University, Cambridge, MA. 1993. Department of Government. Comparative politics with specialization in statistical methodology.

Institut d’Etudes Politiques de Paris, France. 1989–1990. Awarded the Certificat d’Etudes Politiques with Honors. Specialization: contemporary European politics.

B.A., University of South Carolina, Columbia, SC. 1986–1991. Summa cum laude. Intensive specialization in political science, with Honors from the South Carolina Honors College.
